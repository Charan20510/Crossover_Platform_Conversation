How to set up a new mailbox

  1. Turn on IMAP in your email provider's settings.
  2. Get an app password — see the table below. Your normal password won't work if 2-step verification is on.
  3. Fill the form above — name, address, app password and your provider's hosts. Leave IMAP/SMTP username blank unless your provider differs from your address; ports 993 / 587 suit almost everyone.
  4. Click Add mailbox, then Connect on its row in Mailboxes above — the status turns Connected.
  5. Click Use to make it active, then open Inbox.
